Offcut for Mac

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026.

Offcut is a menu-bar clipboard history. This policy describes the Mac App Store app (bundle identifier com.offcutmac), published by Recrem Digital Pty Ltd.

Clipboard text stays on this Mac. Offcut does not send it to the developer or to any other server. For Apple’s App Privacy questionnaire, Offcut does not collect data.

What stays on your Mac

Offcut reads plain text from the macOS clipboard so it can show a local history. That text is written only to history.json inside the app’s sandbox container, at Application Support/Offcut. On a Mac App Store install, that file is:

~/Library/Containers/com.offcutmac/Data/Library/Application Support/Offcut/history.json

  • Recent history keeps the newest 100 text copies, about 100KB of text each.
  • Pins keep up to 100 copies, each with an optional group name you choose. Pins stay until you unpin them.

Images and files are ignored. Copies that a password manager marks as concealed are ignored and are not stored.

Start at Login only registers Offcut as a macOS login item. It does not send information about your Mac.

What Offcut does not do

  • No accounts, and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash reports.
  • No iCloud or other sync. History is not uploaded.
  • No sale or sharing of clipboard text, because it never leaves your Mac.

How long it is kept

Recent copies stay until newer copies replace them, or until you choose Clear History. Clear History removes recent copies only. Pins stay until you unpin them.

Deleting Offcut’s container data removes history.json. You can do that from Finder, or by removing the app’s data under ~/Library/Containers/com.offcutmac.

Children

Offcut is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If Offcut’s behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
